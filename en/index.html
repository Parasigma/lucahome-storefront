Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered

What is Non-Resident Income Tax?

It is the tax on income earned in Spain by people who are not tax resident in the country. Property owners declare it using Form 210 (Modelo 210), even when the property is not rented out.

How much tax will I have to pay?

It depends on the property's cadastral value, your income and your country of residence (as a general rule, 19% for residents of the EU, Iceland and Norway, and 24% for everyone else). We calculate the exact amount and let you know before we file.

When does the 24-hour period start?

From the moment you submit your complete documentation from your private area. If any document is missing, we'll let you know straight away.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

If we don't file your return within the 24-hour period, we refund the full amount to your original payment method. You don't have to justify anything.

Are my documents safe?

Yes. The connection is encrypted, your files are kept in private storage, and only the experts handling your case can access them.

What if there are several owners?

Each owner must file their own return. Order one service per owner; you can do it all from the same account.
